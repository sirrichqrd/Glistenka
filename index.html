Proposal Overview

A Seminar for the Whole School Community

Self-defence extends beyond physical techniques.

A meaningful personal-safety programme should help people understand how to recognise situations, make safer decisions, communicate effectively, manage pressure and respond appropriately when confronted with a potential threat.

Glisten Karate Club proposes a school-wide Self-Defence & Personal Safety Seminar designed to bring these principles into an accessible and practical learning environment — creating an opportunity for students, teachers, staff, parents and guardians to learn together, and to develop a shared understanding of personal safety.

1. Proposal Overview

Self-defence extends beyond physical techniques. Glisten Karate Club proposes a school-wide Self-Defence & Personal Safety Seminar for the Glisten International Academy community — an interactive learning experience bringing together students, teachers, staff, parents and guardians to develop a shared understanding of personal safety.

A meaningful personal-safety programme helps people recognise situations, make safer decisions, communicate effectively, manage pressure and respond appropriately when confronted with a potential threat. This seminar brings those principles into an accessible, practical setting.

2. Why This Seminar

Many situations can be managed — or avoided entirely — before they become physical confrontations. The seminar therefore equips participants to:

  • Pay attention to their surroundings;
  • Recognise potentially unsafe situations and identify warning signs;
  • Establish and communicate personal boundaries;
  • Make safer decisions under pressure;
  • Use verbal communication to de-escalate situations;
  • Seek appropriate assistance; and
  • Respond appropriately when physical intervention becomes unavoidable.

The emphasis is on awareness, prevention, responsible response and personal confidence — rather than encouraging confrontation.

3. Key Learning Areas

  1. Situational Awareness — understanding the environment, identifying unusual behaviour and recognising potential warning signs.
  2. Prevention & Risk Awareness — how everyday choices, positioning and awareness reduce exposure to unnecessary risk.
  3. Boundaries & Assertive Communication — personal boundaries, and appropriate ways to communicate clearly and confidently.
  4. Verbal De-escalation — how communication, composure and decision-making can prevent situations from escalating.
  5. Protective Responses — simple, practical principles for protecting oneself and creating an opportunity to move away from danger.
  6. Getting Help — when and how to seek assistance from trusted adults, authorities, security personnel or emergency services.
  7. Post-Incident Awareness — what to do after an incident, including reporting, seeking assistance and preserving personal safety.

4. The Seminar Experience

Rather than being a lecture alone, the seminar combines several learning methods:

  • Presentation — clear explanations of personal-safety principles;
  • Demonstration — instructors demonstrate practical concepts in an easy-to-understand format;
  • Guided Participation — safe, controlled exercises where appropriate;
  • Scenario-Based Learning — age-appropriate situations used to explore decision-making and responses;
  • Discussion & Q&A — participants ask questions and discuss real-life concerns.

This makes the programme suitable for a school-wide audience, while allowing activities to be adapted to the age and needs of each group.

5. Target Audience

  • Students — age-appropriate personal safety awareness, confidence, boundaries and practical responses.
  • Teachers & Staff — practical awareness and response principles applicable within and outside the school environment.
  • Parents & Guardians — understanding how to reinforce personal-safety awareness and responsible behaviour at home.
  • The School Community — creating a shared culture of awareness, responsibility and preparedness.

6. School Community Benefits

  • Awareness — more observant, safety-conscious members of the school community;
  • Confidence — calmness and confidence without promoting unnecessary confrontation;
  • Responsibility — understanding the consequences of decisions and actions;
  • Communication — communicating boundaries and seeking help appropriately;
  • Community — a shared understanding of personal safety across students, staff and families.

7. Instruction Team

  • Sensei Isaac — General Lead · Primary Session Instructor. Leads the Glisten Karate Club's primary-session instruction and oversees the school's broader karate programme. (Royal Legendary Martial Art)
  • Sensei Raphael — Secondary Session Instructor. Leads secondary-session delivery, specialising in scenario-based learning and protective responses for older students. (TTOSR)
  • Sempai David — Assistant Instructor · Demonstration Lead. Supports guided practice across groups and leads the demonstration segments. (Bushido Martial Art Academy)

Sessions are supported by the club's instructional affiliations: TTOSR, Bushido Martial Art Academy, FCT Karate Association, Nigerian Karate Association and the Karate Federation of Nigeria.

All practical activities are age-appropriate, non-competitive and conducted on controlled surfaces. Participation in physical segments is optional and supervised at all times.

9. Proposed Programme Flow

  1. Welcome & Opening Brief (≈10 min) — team introductions, purpose and ground rules.
  2. Awareness Talk (≈15 min) — situational awareness, boundaries and everyday risk awareness.
  3. Live Demonstration (≈15 min) — awareness in action, de-escalation and protective responses.
  4. Guided Practice (≈20 min) — safe, controlled exercises adapted to each age group.
  5. Scenario Discussions (≈10 min) — age-appropriate situations exploring decisions and responses.
  6. Q&A & Reflection (≈10 min) — open floor for questions and real-life concerns.
  7. Closing & Safety Pledge (≈5 min) — key takeaways and a shared personal-safety pledge.

Timings are indicative and will be adjusted to the final group structure agreed with Management.
